# D1 plan: v2 visual design

Source: `docs/DESIGN.md`. Scope: the Sprout character (replaces the PlantFaceiOS face) and
the restyled PlantMonitor home screen with the day, thirsty and night scenes.

Out of scope: the tab bar, the Care, Moods and Garden screens, the streak pill, the settings
button, and the real Talk feature. The speech bubble shows a template line per mood until M5.
The debug Water button stays.

## 1. Where things go

| Piece | Target / folder |
|---|---|
| `PlantFaceState` (happy, thirsty, hot, cold, asleep, overwatered) + mood mapping | PlantFace (business). Replaces `PlantFaceEyes`. |
| Sprout drawing, poses, effects, motion | PlantFaceiOS. Keeps the `PlantFaceView` / `PlantFaceUIComposer` names, so the app and PlantMonitor's `Face` slot don't change. |
| `PlantMonitorScene` (day, thirsty, night), `PlantMonitorSceneViewModel` (scene, chip sensors, warnings) | PlantMonitor/Presentation. Deterministic, unit-tested. |
| Header text, speech lines, Talk / Water labels | PlantMonitor/Presentation + `PlantMonitor.strings` |
| Palettes, scene card, chips, bubble, buttons | PlantMonitoriOS |

No new targets, no new dependencies.

## 2. Sprout character (PlantFace / PlantFaceiOS)

**PlantFace:** `PlantFaceViewModel(mood:)` exposes:

- `state`: happy → happy; thirsty, veryThirsty, dryAir → thirsty; tooHot → hot;
  tooCold → cold; tooDark, offline → asleep; drowning → overwatered.
- `drySoil`: true for thirsty and veryThirsty only. It drives the dry soil color and the pot
  cracks, so dryAir gets the thirsty pose with normal soil and no cracks (it isn't a watering
  problem).
- `extraTilt`: 6° for veryThirsty, 0 otherwise.
- `blinks`: true when the eyes are open (happy, thirsty, overwatered).

`PlantFaceEyes` and the old properties (`eyes`, `mouthCurve`, `isMouthOpen`) are removed.

**PlantFaceiOS:**

- **Drawing:** drawn in the 240 × 280 viewBox and scaled uniformly to 270 × 315. Each element
  is a SwiftUI `Shape` with a hand-written `Path` (move, line, quad, curve), drawn back to
  front in the order of DESIGN 5.1. No SVG parser.
  - Leaves use one `PlantSproutLeafShape` + vein, placed with offset, `rotationEffect` and
    `scaleEffect` around their origin.
  - The head group rotates by `tilt + extraTilt` around (120, 206).
- **Poses:** `PlantFacePose` (style struct, one static value per state) holds every number in
  DESIGN 5.2: colors, tilt, top leaf angles / scale / y, arm angles / scale, eye ry, blush,
  mouth fill. Soil is overwatered → wet soil, `drySoil` → dry soil, otherwise default soil.
- **Morphing:** `.animation(spring ~0.6 s, value: state)`, so leaf rotations, tilt, scales,
  colors and soil animate. Eyes, brows, mouth and effects are subviews keyed by state with
  `.transition(.opacity)`, so they cross-fade.
  - The face keeps the same view identity across moods (no `.id()`). Otherwise the morph
    restarts from scratch.
- **Effects (DESIGN 5.3):** sparkles, pot cracks (only when `drySoil`), sweat, shiver lines,
  Zzz, drops and puddle. Where DESIGN only gives rough positions, I choose the exact shapes:
  - a teardrop of about 10 × 14 for drops
  - three horizontal shiver lines per side at y 108, 128 and 148
- **Idle motion:**
  - A blink every `Double.random(in: 3...6)` s, only when `blinks` is true.
  - Head breathing from 1.0 to 1.02 over 3 s.
  - The top leaves sway ±3°.
  - With Reduce Motion there's no idle motion, and a mood change is a cross-fade of the
    whole character.
- The character stays hidden from accessibility. The header subtitle carries the mood.
- **Removed:** `PlantFaceEyeView`, `PlantFaceMouthShape`, `PlantFaceMouthView`.

## 3. Home screen (PlantMonitor / PlantMonitoriOS)

**`PlantMonitorSceneViewModel(reading: PlantReading?, mood: PlantMood?)`:**

- `scene`:
  - night when lux < 10 or the mood is offline
  - otherwise thirsty when the mood is thirsty or veryThirsty
  - otherwise day (including when there's no data yet)
  - So night beats thirsty, and dryAir stays in the day scene.
- `sensors`: `[moisture, light, humidity]` when the mood is dryAir, otherwise
  `[moisture, light, temperature]`.
- `warnings`: moisture when the mood is thirsty or veryThirsty, humidity when it's dryAir,
  otherwise none.
  - Warnings follow the mood, not the scene, so a warning chip keeps its warning style at
    night too.

**`PlantMonitorViewModel`:**

- Remembers the latest reading and mood.
- Emits `loadedScene` with a `PlantMonitorSceneViewModel` after every reading and every mood.
- Gains `talkTap()`, `waterTap()`, `talkLabel` ("Talk to %@") and `waterLabel` ("Water").
- `PlantMonitorAction` gains `.talkTap` and `.waterTap`.

**New and changed view models:**

- `PlantMoodViewModel` gains `speech` (`plant.monitor.mood.<case>.speech`):

  | Mood | Line |
  |---|---|
  | happy | "Feeling great today!" |
  | thirsty | "I could use a drink soon." |
  | veryThirsty | "Water, please! I'm parched." |
  | drowning | "Too much water! Let me dry out." |
  | tooDark | "It's a bit dark in here." |
  | tooHot | "Phew, it's hot in here." |
  | tooCold | "Brr, I'm a little cold." |
  | dryAir | "The air feels so dry." |
  | offline | "Zzz… I can't feel my sensor." |

- `PlantMonitorHeaderViewModel(name:room:mood:)` gives the `title` and a `subtitle` of
  `"<room> · <mood label>"` (format key `plant.monitor.header.subtitle`), or just the room
  before a mood loads.

**Composer:** `composed(with:healthLoader:plantName:room:face:actionHandler:)`

- On `loadedScene` it sets:
  - the palette
  - the sun or the moon for the window
  - the chips, built from `sensors` and `warnings`
  - the buttons:
    - thirsty scene: Water is primary; Talk and History are secondary
    - otherwise: Talk is primary; Water and History are secondary
- On `loadedMood` it sets the face, the bubble and the subtitle.

**Store:**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `palette` | Default day |
| `subtitle` | Header subtitle text |
| `window` | Sun or moon |
| `face` | The `Face` view (`PlantMoodView` is removed) |
| `bubble` | Speech bubble |
| `chips: [PlantSensorChipView]` | The three sensor chips |
| `primaryButton`, `secondaryButtons` | The action buttons |
| `loading`, `error` | Same as today |

**Views:**

| View | Notes |
|---|---|
| `PlantMonitorView` | ScrollView → header, scene card, actions. Background comes from the palette. The palette reaches subviews through one `@Entry` environment value. The night scene sets `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` so the status bar stays readable. |
| `PlantMonitorHeaderView` | Title and subtitle |
| `PlantMonitorSceneView` | 440 high, radius 32. Floor and sill, window, sprout, bubble, chips, placed as in DESIGN 4. The window anchors from the trailing edge so it stays top-right on every width. |
| `PlantMonitorWindowView` | Frame, panes, sky, sun or crescent moon and 4 stars |
| `PlantSpeechBubbleView` | `UnevenRoundedRectangle`, corners 20 / 20 / 20 / 6 |
| `PlantSensorChipView` | Replaces `PlantSensorCardView` and reuses `PlantSensorCardViewModel`. The warning style is the warning fill, a 2 pt inner border, and warning text colors. Icons: drop, sun, thermometer, humidity. |
| `PlantMonitorButtonView` | Primary pill (56 high) and secondary pill (48 high, 2 pt border in its accent color) |
| Loading skeleton | Three chip-shaped placeholders in the chip row instead of covering the screen. The error overlay is unchanged. |

**Styles:** every color, font and size goes in a style enum.

- `PlantMonitorColor`: `PlantMonitorPalette` with `.day`, `.thirsty` and `.night` (every
  token in DESIGN 2, plus `onPrimary`: white by day, `#0F2418` at night), and the warning
  tokens.
- `PlantMonitorFont`: the 8 rounded styles from DESIGN 3. The bubble text gets about 5 pt
  of line spacing.
- `PlantMonitorSize` (new): every padding, size and radius from DESIGN 4. The chip's inner
  padding (about 10 × 12) isn't in DESIGN, so I choose it.
- `PlantFaceColor`, `PlantFaceLine`, `PlantFaceAnimation` and `PlantFacePose` (new) for the
  character.
- Colors use a 4-line `Color(hex:)` initializer in each Styles folder (PlantFaceiOS,
  PlantMonitoriOS). It's duplicated rather than adding a `PlantSharediOS` target. There's no
  asset catalog. The scene picks the palette, not the system light / dark setting.

**App:**

- Passes `plantName: "Sprout"` and `room: "Living room"`, both hard-coded for now.
- `.talkTap` does nothing until M5.
- `.waterTap` calls `simulatedPlant.water()` in DEBUG and does nothing otherwise.
- The debug toolbar Water button stays.

## Notes

- **Humidity leaves the home screen** except when the mood is dryAir. It stays on History.
- **Fixed font sizes** from DESIGN 3 don't scale with Dynamic Type.
- **Night dark mode:** `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` at night also applies to History while
  it's pushed. History uses adaptive colors, so it just shows in dark mode.
- **What the simulator shows:**
  - Light is 0 lx from 18:00 to 06:00 in simulated time, so with the default time scale the
    night scene shows for about one real minute out of every two.
  - Simulated humidity stays between 45 and 55 %, so dryAir (below 35 %) never fires there.
    The dryAir pose and the humidity chip are covered by tests only.
