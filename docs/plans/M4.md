## 2. Profile (`PlantProfile.houseplant`)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `thirstyMoisture` | < 30 % |
| `veryThirstyMoisture` | < 15 % |
| `drowningMoisture` / `drowningDuration` | ≥ 90 % for ≥ 48 h |
| `temperature` | 15…30 °C (below = too cold, above = too hot) |
| `minimumHumidity` | 35 % |
| `minimumAverageLux` / `lightWindow` / `minimumLightCoverage` | average ≥ 200 lx over 24 h, judged only once there are at least 18 h of readings |
| `offlineAfter` | 15 min (3 × the normal 300 s interval in BLE_CONTRACT.md) |
| `historyWindow` | the longest window any rule needs (48 h) |

These values fit the simulator. Right after watering it reads 100 % and drops below 90 % in
about 7 h, so watering never triggers drowning. It reaches 30 % after about 4 min of real
time and 15 % after about 5 min. That covers "the face changes as the plant dries out".

## 3. Rules (`PlantHealthEvaluator.evaluate(readings:now:profile:) -> PlantHealth`)

`readings` covers the history window, oldest first. "Latest" means the newest reading. A
rule whose value is `nil` is skipped.

- **offline:** no readings, or `now − latest.receivedAt > offlineAfter`.
- **drowning:** the latest reading is very wet, and the current wet streak is at least
  `drowningDuration` long. The streak starts at the first very-wet reading after the last
  reading below the threshold, or at the oldest reading if every reading is very wet. So
  drowning can't fire until we actually have 48 h of wet history.
- **very thirsty / thirsty:** latest moisture below 15 % / 30 % (mutually exclusive).
- **too hot / too cold:** latest temperature outside 15…30 °C.
- **too dark:** average lux of the readings in the last 24 h is below 200, and the oldest of
  those readings is at least 18 h old. This way 0 lx at night doesn't count, and neither
  does a fresh install.
- **dry air:** latest humidity below 35 %.

**Priority** (issues are sorted by it, and the mood is the first issue, or happy when there
are none):
offline > drowning > very thirsty > too hot > too cold > thirsty > too dark > dry air.

**Score:** 100 minus the sum of penalties, floored at 0. Penalties: very thirsty 60,
drowning 60, too hot 40, too cold 40, thirsty 30, too dark 25, dry air 15, offline 0.
Offline gives no penalty because the score reflects the plant's last known condition. The
offline mood already signals that the data is stale.

## 4. Loader and view model

```swift
@MainActor public protocol PlantHealthLoader {
    typealias Result = Swift.Result<PlantHealth, Error>
    func load(completion: @escaping (Result) -> Void)
    func subscribe(update: @escaping () -> Void)   // "evaluate again"
}
```

- **`LocalPlantHealthLoader(store:profile:currentDate:recheckInterval:schedule:)`**
  - `load` fetches `now − historyWindow … now` from the store and evaluates. A store
    failure becomes `.retrieval`.
  - `subscribe` forwards the store's insert notifications and also fires every
    `recheckInterval` through the injected `schedule`. Offline needs this, because no new
    reading arrives to trigger an evaluation.
  - `schedule` has no default, so PlantMonitor doesn't depend on the simulator. The app
    passes `SimulatedPeripheralClient.repeatingTimer`.
  - Callbacks never capture `self` (memory-leak test included).
- **`PlantMonitorViewModel`** gains a `healthLoader` and a `loadedMood: Observer<PlantMood>`.
  - `appear()` loads health and subscribes, reloading on each update, the same way
    `PlantHistoryViewModel` does. Repeated appears are still guarded.
  - On a load failure it keeps the last mood. Reading errors already have their own UI.
- **`PlantMoodViewModel(mood:)`** exposes a localised `label`. New keys go in
  `PlantMonitor.strings`: `plant.monitor.mood.<case>.label` ("Happy", "Thirsty",
  "Very thirsty", "Drowning", "Too dark", "Too hot", "Too cold", "Dry air", "Offline").

## 5. UI

- **PlantMonitoriOS**
  - `PlantMonitorUIComposer.composed(with:healthLoader:face:actionHandler:) -> PlantMonitorView<Face>`,
    matching ARCHITECTURE.md 5.4.
  - The store becomes generic and holds `@Published var mood: PlantMoodView<Face>?`.
    `PlantMoodView` is the face with the label under it.
  - It sits centred at the top of the scroll view, above the cards.
- **PlantFace**
  - `PlantFaceViewModel(mood:)` exposes `eyes` (open, wide, droopy, squint, closed),
    `mouthCurve` (−1 frown … 1 smile), `isMouthOpen` and `blinks`.
  - Offline means closed eyes and no blinking, so the plant looks asleep.
- **PlantFaceiOS**
  - `PlantFaceView` with an eye view and a `PlantFaceMouthShape` whose curve is animatable,
    so mood changes morph smoothly.
  - Idle animation: a blink every few seconds plus a slight "breathing" scale, both off when
    Reduce Motion is on. The face is hidden from accessibility because the label carries
    the mood.
  - `PlantFaceUIComposer.composed(mood:) -> PlantFaceView`.
- **App:** wires up `LocalPlantHealthLoader` with the same store and the simulated clock,
  and passes `PlantFaceUIComposer.composed` as `face`. `Package.swift` gets the 2 new
  targets, the 2 new test targets and the products.

The human runs the iOS integration tests and does the visual check.

## Notes

- **Offline won't show in the simulator.** In simulated mode, "now" is the simulated clock,
  and that clock always equals the time of the latest reading. Offline is unit-tested and
  becomes visible with the real board (M6).
- **The dark and drowning rules wait for history.** Too dark needs 18 h of readings and
  drowning needs 48 h, so neither can fire on a fresh install.
